The Catch

Net / Jacket / Skirt

Inspired by Bahrain's pearl-diving heritage during Sheikh Isa Al-Kabeer's era, this look represents the sea as a source of prosperity and identity. The draped beige skirt reflects the traditional garments and clothing associated with Bahrain's heritage, while the netted layer across the hips is inspired by the nets used in pearl diving. The sheer light-blue jacket represents the sea, creating a visual connection to the waters that sustained Bahrain's pearl industry. The combination of these elements with a contemporary silhouette reflects EZDR's approach to bringing Bahrain's heritage into modern streetwear

Reworked

Cap / Shirt / Jeans

Inspired by Bahrain's heritage during Sheikh Isa Al-Kabeer's era, this look explores how prosperity grows when the past becomes part of the future. The traditional Wazar-inspired fabric emerges through the ripped sides of the denim, deliberately showing the old breaking through the new. The denim represents modern streetwear and a contemporary Bahrain, while the exposed traditional fabric preserves its cultural roots. Pearls incorporated into the cap reference Bahrain's pearl-diving industry, an important source of prosperity during the era. Together, these elements express EZDR's vision of reimagining heritage rather than replacing it.

First Lesson

Blazer / Shirt / Tie / Pants

Inspired by the development of education during Sheikh Isa AI-Kabeer's era, this look represents knowledge as a foundation for prosperity. The white shirt, collar and tie draw from traditional formal menswear associated with education and professionalism, while the oversized sleeveless blazer and relaxed trousers transform these elements into contemporary streetwear. The Arabic lettering incorporated into the burgundy necktie represents language, knowledge and the growth of education, connecting the look directly to Bahrain's intellectual development. The contrast between traditional tailoring and a modern silhouette reflects a society moving forward while carrying its foundations with it.
