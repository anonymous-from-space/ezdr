03 / From the studio

A little
more human.

Every garment begins as a question: what would make this feel better to live in? Not just look good in. We care about the bend in an elbow, the weight of a pocket, and what happens after the tenth wash.

That is the work. The rest is just getting dressed.

— the ezdr studio
